01 / The Genesis story

From first connection
to living community.

Genesis is a developing city on the Lakeland map of Cities: Skylines II, built directly by its Founder. A founding Mayor and five independently minded councilors form the municipal administration, advising on infrastructure, budgets, neighborhoods, environmental stewardship, and the long-term vision.

Our earliest physical achievement is the high-voltage transmission approach and its first transformer station. The city remains at the infrastructure-building stage; residents, service commissioning, and further projects will follow only when justified by the simulation and approved by the Founder.

02 / Principles of governance

Liberty with responsibility.

01 / Freedom

People first.

Social liberty, equal treatment, entrepreneurship, and room for future citizens to live as they choose.

02 / Discipline

Know the costs.

Sound fiscal stewardship, transparent accounting, and no confusion between proposed spending and verified outlays.

03 / Stewardship

Protect the future.

Dependable public works, thoughtful roads, accessible lakeshore, and a municipal institution designed to endure.
