01 / Our story

From first connection
to lasting community.

Genesis begins on the Lakeland map in Cities: Skylines II. The Founder personally builds the city; a small municipal government considers strategy, finance, infrastructure, the environment, and the needs of future residents.

Our first undertaking established the initial transmission connection and transformer station near the waterfront approach. We will expand deliberately, so each new district has a foundation to rely on.

02 / Municipal philosophy

What we stand for.

Founder-directed civic government with a socially liberal, economically libertarian outlook: personal freedom alongside accountable essential services.

01 — LIBERTY

Room to live.

Personal autonomy, an open civic culture, equal treatment, and economic opportunity for future citizens.

02 — STEWARDSHIP

Spend with reason.

Verified figures, phased projects, sustainable upkeep, and a clear record of what has actually been built.

03 — LEGACY

Protect tomorrow.

Reliable utilities and transport, carefully stewarded shoreline, and institutions built to outlast their first term.

03 / The governing framework

Responsibilities and limits

THE FOUNDER

Reserved authority

Final approval for major construction, foundational legislation, large financial obligations and the future governance framework.

THE MAYOR

Executive leadership

Municipal planning, disciplined administration, preparation of projects, and honest status reporting, without unilateral in-game building.

THE COUNCIL

Independent scrutiny

Review of proposals, budgets, services, infrastructure and environmental questions. Votes are recorded only when an actual deliberation occurs.
